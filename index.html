How to play

  • Pieces move like in chess, but there are no captures - moving onto an enemy piece swaps places with it
  • A piece that was just swapped can't swap straight back
  • Win: line up four of your own pieces - across, down or diagonally, four in a row with no gaps
  • A swap does two jobs: it pulls your piece into a line and knocks theirs out of one
  • White's first move must be a knight move onto an empty square - the handicap that makes going first fair
  • No repeats: you can't make a move that brings back a position already seen this game (unless it wins). If you have no other move, it's a draw

Cardinal Chess Rules

  • Pieces move like standard chess
  • No capturing - pieces swap positions
  • Can't swap back immediately after being swapped
  • Four of your own pieces in a line wins - across, down or diagonally, no gaps
  • A swap can complete both lines at once; the player who moved wins
  • White's first move must be a knight move onto an empty square
  • No move may bring back a position already seen this game, unless it wins - banned moves are greyed out
  • No legal move left = draw
