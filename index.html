How to play — Classic

  • Pieces move like in chess, but there are no captures - moving onto an enemy piece swaps places with it
  • A piece that was just swapped can't swap straight back
  • Score by the piece you swap with: Knight 5, Bishop/King 3, Rook 2, Queen 1
  • Win: swaps add to your score - first to 25 points wins
  • Threefold repetition is a draw

Cardinal Chess Rules

  • Pieces move like standard chess
  • No capturing - pieces swap positions
  • Can't swap back immediately after being swapped
  • A swap scores by the piece you moved: Knight 5, Bishop/King 3, Rook 2, Queen 1
  • Swaps add to your score - first to 25 points wins
  • Threefold repetition = automatic draw
